City Infrastructure

City Connectors

When a city has a measured area, you can build a Connector in it. Connectors link cities to each other and to your Army base, forming a connected network.

Armies marching along these routes do not consume resources, making them vital for efficient expansion.

Building requires 150 medals. Each Connector has a usage limit of 20 marches before it expires.

City Hall

The City Hall can be built in a measured city, providing a 20% bonus to defense and a 10% boost to gold income.

To construct it, you must first occupy the city and locate its true position within the urban area.

Construction Cost: 25 Medals

Durability

  • Maximum durability is 5.
  • Each time the city is captured by another player, the City Hall loses 1 durability.
  • If durability reaches 0, the City Hall is destroyed and must be rebuilt for 25 Medals.

If you voluntarily leave the city, the City Hall remain in place and do not lose durability.

Increase Durability

You can increase the City Hall's durability by 1 at the following resource cost:

  • Oil: 70,000
  • Steel: 70,000
  • Rare: 25,000

Monuments

Monuments are built to commemorate major battles that took place in a city. The victorious side can choose a location within the city’s measured area to construct a Monument, leaving a lasting mark of their triumph.

If you occupy a city containing Monuments, you will receive 50 Gold per day for each Monument in that city.

Monuments also contribute to your Achievement Points and may give you the opportunity to earn a Custom Title.

Fortifications

Fortifications can be built in a measured city, providing a 30% defense bonus against enemy armies/navies and a 50% defense bonus against enemy air forces/missiles for all stationed armies or navies.

Construction Cost: 25 Medals.

Durability

  • Maximum durability is 5.
  • Each time the city is captured by another player, the Fortifications lose 1 durability.
  • If durability reaches 0, the Fortifications are destroyed and must be rebuilt for 25 Medals.

If you voluntarily leave the city, the Fortifications remain in place and do not lose durability.

Increase Durability

You can increase the Fortifications' durability by 1 at the following resource cost:

  • Oil: 80,000
  • Steel: 80,000
  • Rare: 15,000
