Overview

Weapons are essential for combat. You must equip your armies, navies, and air forces with weapons in order to compete with other players and conquer cities.

Types of Weapons

  • Firearms & Artillery
  • Tanks
  • Ships
  • Aircraft
  • Missiles

Production

  • Weapons are produced using resources and gold.
  • Produced weapons are automatically stored in your weapon inventory.

Repair

  • Weapons damaged in battle are moved to the Repair Storage.
  • Repairing a weapon costs half the resources and gold required to produce a new one.
